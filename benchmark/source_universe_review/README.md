# Full source metadata and hydantoin/nitrile review

This is source triage, with **zero admitted independent references** and no new
predictions. It preserves the existing primary benchmark rules.

The complete 5,735-row metadata projection contains 897 strict named equations.
After the declared name/phase/typography exposure screen, 518 equation groups
remain leads. Ten have any row with all four condition fields filled; none of
those rows is nominally 298.15 K. These counts do not establish eligibility,
chemical novelty or measurement independence. A blank Mg field never means absent
Mg, and phase removal is an exposure alias only.

Two additional papers were reviewed using their official NIST abstracts,
publisher metadata and the owned NIST Part7 evaluated compilation. The publisher
API returned HTTP200 records containing core metadata only; neither original
article body was obtained. `source_review.json` distinguishes these access levels.

The hydantoin study has finite equilibria, including chemical-reference constants
reported by the evaluated compilation. Its dihydrouracil reaction already occurs
in comparator training and historical MetaG inputs. The achiral hydantoin graphs
have close training neighbours; aryl variants remain possible structural leads
with unresolved stereochemical identity and condition/uncertainty derivation.
The shared source and hydrolysis family cannot supply ten independent clusters.

The nitrile study supplies lower equilibrium bounds and calorimetric enthalpies.
Neither is admitted as a point Gibbs-energy target. A product-name conflict in
projected row5534 is flagged against both official abstract and evaluated paper;
the original input is preserved. A later bounds-based validation would require a
separate declared endpoint and authenticated bound/condition construction.

`source_replay.zip` holds the outcome-free structural projections, historical input
union, exact research scripts and existing family-normalization source. The two
compressed reports retain all screened leads and unresolved structures. The
unassigned aryl stereocentres remain unresolved; the graph hypotheses cannot be
used as experimentally authenticated scoring inputs. Similarities use the entire
known original pool corpus, including formation targets, without fitting or
training exclusions. Unknown training structures preclude certified absence.

Run `python benchmark/replay_source_universe.py` from the MetaG repository to
reconstruct both reports in an isolated temporary layout without inference or
network access. Replay verifies metadata/structural arithmetic, not the primary
articles' experimental validity. Raw article copies stay in the owned study;
public source URLs and byte hashes identify what was actually inspected.
