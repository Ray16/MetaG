# Independent-reference curation, 4 November 2026

No experimental reaction is admitted by this packet. The original validation
design and its launch prohibition remain in effect. These records narrow the
source and identity work needed before an independent comparison; they contain
no new MetaG or dGbyG predictions, residuals, or fitted corrections.

The aromatic transamination hypotheses pass the structural screen on both
retained concrete training corpora. Each contains three absent compound families
whose nearest chiral Morgan similarity is below 0.5. This does not resolve
experimental conditions, measurement ancestry, comparator checkpoint ownership,
or abstract training labels. The R/S alternatives for the 2012 source are
hypotheses, not two experimental observations. The methyl-ferulate hypothesis
does not meet the distant-chemistry threshold, and the primary 2011 experiment
specifies cis/trans mixtures with unknown fractions.

The 2024 hydride-transfer candidate illustrates why reference curation matters.
Its inherited reverse kinetic constants refer to reactive carbonyl forms.
Using the cited glycolaldehyde and DHAP carbonyl fractions changes the inferred
Gibbs energy by 4.995 kJ/mol when converting to total hydration pools. This is
conditional reference arithmetic, not a validated new label or a correction to
MetaG. Original fraction uncertainties, speciation and activity bridges remain
unresolved. The 2015 broader transamination panel used 5% DMSO at 30 °C under its
general protocol; those observations cannot silently label the pure-water target.

The reusable audit retains concrete molecules on both sides of partially
abstract training equations. It lists wildcard terms separately, normalizes
reaction extent and direction, and preserves molecular stereochemistry. It
never certifies experimental enrollment. From the MetaG repository:

```bash
python benchmark/audit_independent_candidates.py \
  benchmark/state_function_validation/curation_20261104/candidate_chemical_hypotheses.json \
  --training public671=/path/to/public/TrainingData.csv \
  --training author675=/path/to/author/TrainingData.csv \
  --output /path/to/new_candidate_audit.json
```

`status.json` gives the training hashes and source URL for the 675-row table.
The training files and primary full texts are not redistributed here. Different
local input paths change the recorded paths but not the chemical conclusions
when the source hashes and RDKit version match. The JSON manifest authenticates
the copied curation artifacts; it is not evidence that admission gates passed.
